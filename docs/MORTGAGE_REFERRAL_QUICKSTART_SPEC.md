# Mortgage Referral Quick Start — Master Specification
Status: Approved conversation decisions; implementation pending
Owner: New Eglinton / Primerica team
Integration: TrainingBuilder; existing 8Twelve Mortgage Conversation Kit

## Mission
Help brand-new team members become trusted, recognizable mortgage contacts who build a network of people willing to introduce others. Balance relationships with clear appointment-setting and income opportunity. Keep the training simple, conversational, and actionable.

## Non-negotiable decisions
- Phase 1: one-on-one onboarding, 10 hours across two target days (5 hours/day), with real calls on both days.
- First two weeks: senior trainer/partner actively supports the trainee; after onboarding, coaching is on demand.
- Primary audience for initial outreach: friends and family as *introducers*, not immediate product prospects.
- Phone and in-person first; text only as a fallback or for practical coordination.
- Weekly goal: 10–20 genuine referral-network conversations; initial milestone: book and deliver 3 trainer-attended practice Zooms.
- Trainer attends **all three** practice Zooms. First trainer leads, second trainer assists, third trainer observes/coaches; trainee is increasingly the main voice.
- Existing four-page Mortgage Conversation Kit is the source of presentation content. Client-facing pages 1–2 are the default presentation. The existing recruiting/opportunity page is shown only when relevant; verify exact page mapping against the current kit rather than inventing content.
- Client presentation includes a natural optional expansion invitation: 'We're expanding our team. If you know anyone who might be a good fit, I'd be happy to connect with them.' If interest arises, use the existing opportunity page.
- No formal graduation paperwork; trainer verbally checks readiness.
- Phase 2: separate build of a personalized, mobile-first, shareable introduction webpage for each trainee; **not** a Phase 1 prerequisite.

## Positioning and voice
Plain, friendly Canadian English. Avoid hype, pressure, heavy disclaimers and the word 'business' in outreach; say 'I'm growing my clientele.' The trainee is the ongoing contact and owns the relationship, not a person merely passing names. 8Twelve is a distinct licensed mortgage brokerage whose specialists handle mortgage advice/application work. Describe the specialist as part of the extended team without implying shared legal ownership or licensing. Do not promise rates, approvals, income or guaranteed outcomes. 'Considering getting licensed later' may be said **only if true for that individual**.

## Approved outreach flow (adapt naturally)
1. Genuine reconnect: 'Hey, how've you been? What's new?'
2. Transition: 'Anyway, the reason I'm calling is I've started getting involved in mortgages and wanted to share what I'm doing.'
3. Explain: 'I'm growing my clientele and want to become someone people think of when mortgages come up. I'm working with one of the senior guys on our team, and we have a relationship with 8Twelve. Their mortgage specialists take care of the mortgage side, and I help connect people and stay involved.'
4. Practice context: 'For the next couple of weeks, my senior partner is showing me the ropes and sitting in while I practise.'
5. Single ask: 'Could we do a quick 15- or 20-minute Zoom? I'll walk you through what I'm doing, with him there to guide me. Then you'll know how I can help if you or someone you know ever needs mortgage help. Would Thursday evening work?'
6. If asked 'Why the trainer?': 'I'm just getting started and getting my feet wet. He's showing me how to do this properly, almost like job shadowing, so I can do it confidently myself.'
7. If asked 'Are you getting licensed?': explain the current nonlicensed introduction role accurately; mention potential future licensing only if true.
8. If the contact is not interested: respond courteously, don't pressure; ask permission to remain a contact for future mortgage needs when appropriate.
The goal is a **real appointment**, not merely a vague promise to remember the trainee.

## Day 1 — 5 hours (one-on-one)
- 45 min: purpose, realistic income/referral model and role boundaries; no income guarantees.
- 45 min: relationship map (friends/family as introducers); identify first 10–20 warm contacts.
- 60 min: trainer demo call from trainer's own contacts; debrief transition, tone, appointment ask.
- 60 min: practice natural conversations, company/role questions and objections.
- 90 min: trainee makes real phone calls with trainer beside them; book practice Zooms; log outcomes.
Checkpoint: trainee can explain their role, transition naturally and ask for a specific Zoom time.

## Day 2 — 5 hours (one-on-one)
- 45 min: debrief calls; fix awkward phrasing.
- 60 min: rehearse the **existing** Mortgage Conversation Kit client pages 1–2 (no duplicate deck).
- 45 min: practise referral-network close, optional team-expansion invite and recruiting-page conditional use.
- 60 min: practise first client contact: ask light 'buying, renewing or refinancing?' and timing questions, then coordinate a personal introduction with trainer and licensed 8Twelve specialist.
- 90 min: live calls and scheduling with trainer; practise phone/Zoom introductions and follow-up.
Checkpoint: trainee can book, open, present, close, and explain next steps with trainer support.

## Standard 15–20 minute practice Zoom
- Trainee welcomes and explains their goal: growing clientele and learning with senior partner.
- Trainee presents **existing client pages 1–2**, trainer supports without taking over.
- Explain how a person can reach the trainee when buying, renewing or refinancing comes up.
- Ask: 'If you come across someone who could use mortgage help, would you feel comfortable introducing us?'
- Where natural, mention team expansion and optionally show the existing recruiting page **only** if there is interest.
- Agree on next step; thank participant. Don't force an opportunity pitch on every person.
- Trainer gives a short private debrief after each session.

## Three-Zoom coaching progression
Zoom 1: trainer demonstrates/steers, trainee owns opening and closing.
Zoom 2: trainee delivers most of the content, trainer assists.
Zoom 3: trainee leads, trainer observes and steps in only as needed.
All three include trainer attendance. Completion is based on confidence and capability, not perfection or automatic independence.

## Referral prospect handoff
When an introduction arrives, trainee speaks with prospect first; ask only light context (buy/renew/refinance and timeline). With consent, arrange personal phone or Zoom introduction including trainer and licensed mortgage specialist; complete the existing referral form through the approved process. Avoid discussing rates, suitability, approvals or application advice outside licensed scope. Trainee follows up as the relationship contact.

## Objection practice
Train brief natural answers to: 'What company?', 'Are you licensed?', 'Why is your trainer there?', 'I don't know anyone', 'I already have a mortgage broker', 'Are you selling me something?', 'Can you just text me?', 'I'm too busy', 'Why should I refer you?' Use acknowledge → answer directly → offer a suitable next step; never argue.

## Follow-up and scorecard
After Zoom: short thank-you that night, call/check-in around two days later if a next step is pending, and one gentle follow-up about a week later if an introduction was discussed. Long-term relationship touches around days 30, 60 and 90: **call first**, text as fallback; be useful, not repetitive.
Minimal tracker: contact, last conversation, appointment date, Zoom completed, agreed future introduction, actual introductions, prospect handoff, next follow-up. Track weekly 10–20 conversations and 3 early practice Zooms. Respect consent and applicable contact/privacy rules.

## Trainer responsibilities
- Explain the opportunity and role accurately, model tone and live calls, coach rather than take ownership of relationships.
- Attend all 3 practice Zooms, progressively reduce airtime.
- Ensure presentation matches the approved Mortgage Conversation Kit.
- Support real introductions and licensed specialist handoffs.
- Give 5-minute debriefs and verbal readiness check after approximately two weeks.
- Remain available on demand; do not create unnecessary mandatory weekly meetings.

## Phase 2 — Shareable personal introduction webpage (separate issue)
One standardized mobile webpage per trainee, trainee as the primary face:
1. Hero: photo, name, 'Your connection to mortgage help', Contact and Share buttons.
2. When to reach out: buying, renewing, refinancing.
3. Credibility: senior partner and relationship with licensed 8Twelve mortgage specialists, accurate role description.
4. Simple 'How it works': contact trainee → personal introduction → licensed specialist handles mortgage work.
A single forwardable URL, no PDF or multi-piece kit in MVP. Use approved branding, permissions, privacy and compliance review. No Phase 1 dependency.

## Build acceptance criteria
- Trainer-facing guide and trainee quick-start card derived from this spec, without changing approved voice or scripts.
- Existing kit referenced, not recreated; verify which of its four pages are client/opportunity content.
- Simple tracking and practice-Zoom workflow, with trainer at all 3.
- Live-call exercises, weekly activity targets, phone-first follow-up, and verbal readiness check included.
- Phase 2 explicitly separate, with per-trainee shareable URL design.
- Validate claims and licensing/marketing requirements before external publication. Missing implementation details should trigger a user question or GitHub follow-up, not silent invention.
