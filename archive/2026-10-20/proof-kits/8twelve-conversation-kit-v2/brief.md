**Brand:** 8twelve

**Slug:** 8twelve-conversation-kit-v2

## Why this kit exists
Rebuild the approved seven-sheet 8Twelve conversation kit in the reusable block system and prove that the system reproduces the same content and design. If it matches, future kits can use the same engine.

## Audience
- Page 1: client or prospect.
- Page 2: client or prospect, establishing brokerage credibility and the introduction.
- Page 3: agent or potential teammate exploring the service opportunity.
- Page 4: someone exploring the business opportunity.
- Page 5: advisor, used during a real conversation.
- Page 6: client and advisor, as a handwriting hand-off.
- Page 7: reader of the illustrations, assumptions and sources.

- The named hand-off consent and privacy note.

## Compliance notes
- 8Twelve is a separate licensed mortgage brokerage.
- The advisor identifies needs and introduces; a licensed specialist discusses rates, suitability and approval.
- Business-building belongs to the advisor's own financial brokerage.
- No income, approval, client or success guarantees.
- No real client data in the kit.
