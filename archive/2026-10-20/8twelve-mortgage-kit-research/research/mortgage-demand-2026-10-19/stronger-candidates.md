# Stronger mortgage-agent recruiting candidates
Researched 2026-10-19 in response to a request for more compelling demand statistics. The owner approved these replacements; they are now applied to page 3, with numbered citations and direct original-source links in the disclaimer section.

## Recommended three-card story

1. **$912 billion in mortgage activity in one year.** CMHC's August 27, 2026 Quarterly Data Snapshot reports total mortgage value extended of $912 billion for Q2 2025–Q1 2026 inclusive. This is market-wide lending activity, not agent revenue or agent-channel volume. The source labels it mortgage value extended; do not call it new purchases alone.
2. **70% sought professional mortgage guidance.** CMHC 2025 Mortgage Consumer Survey, p.12: 70% of recent mortgage consumers contacted mortgage professionals to learn about options, up from60%2024. Professionals include lenders and the broker channel. Do not label this70% agent use. Among first-time buyers the contact rate was81%, a narrower denominator.
3. **48% of recent first-time buyers used the broker channel.** MPC/Bond 2026 Broker Advantage, p.3, reports48% for recent first-time buyers and38% for recent buyers overall; survey February5–25,2026. The chart discusses2025 activity. Retain the source's broker-channel label in the source note; the survey is not an agent-only headcount or shortage measure.

Alternative market-scale headline: **$2.4 trillion** household mortgage debt outstanding in June2026 (exact$2.442trillion), CMHC August2026 snapshot. Annual activity is more directly useful for showing repeated transactions; outstanding debt is a stock, not annual lending.
