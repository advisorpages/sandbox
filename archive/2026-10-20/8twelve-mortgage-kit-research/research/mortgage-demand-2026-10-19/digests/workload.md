# Workload evidence screen
Accessed 2026-10-19.

No defensible Canadian mortgage-broker-specific percentage for paperwork burden was found in this bounded search. This is an evidence gap, not proof that the burden does not exist.

- Ownright Operators Report 2026, published May 26, 2026, surveyed 1,015 real estate professionals (agents, brokerage managers and owners), March 27–April 29, 2026. Its public report/press release says 56% have less client time due to compliance/administration; sample is not a mortgage-broker survey. Reject as a mortgage-broker card. https://ownright.com/blog/company-news/operators-report-2026
- CMP's original Brokers on Lenders 2026 reports recurring broker complaints about document review delays and BDM-underwriter communication, but gives no percentage of brokers burdened by paperwork. Can support qualitative workflow context only. https://www.mpamag.com/ca/best-in-mortgage/best-mortgage-lenders-in-canada-according-to-brokers-brokers-on-lenders/586484
- LendMate advertises 20+ hours saved per week, but this is a vendor promise, not a representative Canadian workload estimate. Exclude. https://lendmate.ca/
- Equifax Australia and IMLA UK have mortgage-broker workload/frustration material, but geography is wrong for a Canadian recruiting claim. Exclude.
- MPC's 2026 Broker Advantage is a potential Canadian **consumer** paperwork-help statistic. Candidate 25% of broker clients chose a broker for help with paperwork requires original report confirmation. It must never be restated as 25% of brokers dislike paperwork.

Searches: Canadian mortgage broker paperwork survey; administrative time/burden; Filogix and Newton surveys; Canadian broker workload. Two rounds stopped at novelty exhaustion: results were vendor promises, foreign surveys, or broader real-estate populations.
