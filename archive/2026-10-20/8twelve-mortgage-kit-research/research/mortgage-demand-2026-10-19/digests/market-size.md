# Canadian mortgage market size — evidence digest

Accessed: 2026-10-19. Research limited to retrieved sources this run; approximately five source reads, five web calls. These are defensible dated observations, not a claim to have located every newer release.

## Recommended household-count claim

**Canada had approximately 6.57 million households owning their principal residence with a mortgage in Q2 2025.** Exact estimate: 6,568,430, January 2026 release vintage.

- Publisher: Statistics Canada.
- Source: *Distributions of Household Economic Accounts, estimates of asset, liability and net worth distributions, 2010 to 2025, technical methodology and quality report*, Chart 3 data table and section 6.2.4.
- URL: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/13-604-m/13-604-m2026001-eng.htm
- Published: January 29, 2026. Data period: Q2 2025. Accessed: 2026-10-19.
- Scope/caveats: estimated **households**, grouped by principal-residence tenure; not individual people, unique homeowners, accounts, annual originations, or all-property mortgage loans. The chart compares release vintages: use the January 2026 column (6,568,430), not the older October 2025 estimate (6,635,554). Counts are subject to revisions and recalibrated weights. Not verified as latest available household count.
- Confidence: high for exact dated estimate and denominator.
- Presentation wording: “Millions of households need mortgage advice: about 6.6 million Canadian households owned their home with a mortgage (Q2 2025).” The need-for-advice phrase is an interpretation, not a measured advice-demand statistic.

## Current balance claim

**Canadian household mortgage debt outstanding was $2.442 trillion in June 2026**, versus $2.343 trillion in June 2025 (+4.24%).

- Publisher: Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC), *Residential Mortgage Industry Report*, Quarterly Data Snapshot.
- URL: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/housing-markets-data-and-research/housing-research/research-reports/housing-finance/residential-mortgage-industry-report
- Snapshot publication: August 27, 2026. Data: June 2026. Accessed: 2026-10-19.
- Underlying source named by CMHC: Statistics Canada Table 36-10-0639-01, Credit liabilities of households. This underlying table was not separately retrieved.
- Scope/caveats: dollars of outstanding debt, not mortgage counts, people or annual lending volume. The page combines spring report text and newer snapshot figures; the June 2026 figure belongs to the August snapshot.
- Confidence: high for CMHC's published figure; underlying table not independently checked.
- Presentation wording: “A $2.4 trillion mortgage market (household debt outstanding, June 2026).”

## Independent scale corroboration with a different denominator

The Bank of Canada's RESL2 dataset contained **about six million mortgages and C$1.7 trillion outstanding as of September 2024**. Federally regulated lenders represented about 80% of mortgage stock by value.

- Publisher: Bank of Canada, *Using new loan data to better understand mortgage holders*, Staff Analytical Note 2025-1.
- URL: https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2025/01/staff-analytical-note-2025-1/
- Published: January 2025. Data: September 2024. Accessed: 2026-10-19.
- Scope: federally regulated institutions, including banks and trust companies. Excludes provincially regulated credit unions/caisses populaires, mortgage investment companies, mortgage finance companies and other lenders outside OSFI regulation. Includes standalone mortgages and mortgage components of combined mortgage/HELOC plans.
- Caveats: accounts/loans, not unique mortgaged households. Do **not** divide six million by 80% to estimate national loan count: the 80% denominator is dollar value. Does not independently replicate the Statistics Canada household estimate, but establishes multi-million-loan scale from a separate administrative source.
- Confidence: high for dated dataset description; unsuitable as an exact current national total.

## Source screened but not used for a numeric count

CMHC's Mortgage and Consumer Credit Trends Data Tables page identifies national Equifax-based tables and publication March 25, 2026, but its download link returned the same landing page through the research tool. No count was inferred. The page notes reporting differences affecting trades and consumers, with revised 2019–2022 data.

URL: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/housing-markets-data-and-research/housing-data/data-tables/mortgage-and-debt/mortgage-consumer-credit-trends-cmas

## Recommendation

Use the **6.6 million households** claim when communicating the number of potential household relationships, with Q2 2025 shown. Use **$2.4 trillion outstanding** when communicating current market size. Neither statistic demonstrates advisor earnings, obtainable market share, annual commission opportunity, consumer dissatisfaction, or a guaranteed recruiting outcome.
