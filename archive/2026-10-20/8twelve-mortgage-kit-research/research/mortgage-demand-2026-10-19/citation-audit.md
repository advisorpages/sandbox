# Citation semantic audit — 2026-10-19

Scope: three load-bearing claims in `research.md`, plus page 3 denominator wording in `index.html`. Four web calls; original publishers only. Project text was treated as the claim to test, not factual evidence. No presentation edits made.

- **Statistics Canada: passes.** [Original methodology, Chart 3](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/13-604-m/13-604-m2026001-eng.htm) lists Q2 2025 at 6,568,430 households in the January 2026 vintage, versus 6,635,554 in the older vintage. The surrounding text identifies owners of their principal residence with a mortgage. Page 3's 6.6 million Canadian households with a mortgage in Q2 2025 preserves the unit and date; it is not a count of annual transactions.
- **CMHC: claim and page 3 denominator pass.** [Original 2026 results](https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/housing-markets-data-and-research/housing-research/surveys/mortgage-consumer-surveys/2026-mortgage-consumer-survey), “Mortgage Process Concerns,” directly reports 26% experiencing concerns or uncertainty. This is distinct from the 47% homebuying-process result. The page describes adults making main household decisions who recently completed a mortgage transaction, consistent with “recent mortgage consumers.” **Citation-detail gap:** the linked page supports over 4,100 respondents and January 2026, but its retrieved text does not substantiate the exact 4,112 sample, 18-month eligibility window, or January 7–February 1 fieldwork in `research.md` and the exact sample/date note in `index.html`. Supply an original methodology citation for those details or use the coarser metadata visible on the linked page.
- **MPC/Bond: claim and page 3 denominator pass.** [Original report](https://mortgageproscan.ca/docs/default-source/marcomms/consumer-reports/2026-mpc_the-broker-advantage_en.pdf), printed page 4 (PDF index 3), lists paperwork assistance at 25% under reasons among mortgage borrowers who used a broker. Page 3's “broker clients” is a reasonable compact equivalent. It does not describe brokers' own administrative workload. The report states an online national survey of close to 2,000 Canadians, February 5–25, 2026. The displayed reasons are non-exclusive in aggregate, but the retrieved report text does not explicitly state a select-all question instruction.

Conclusion: no mismatch in any of the three headline figures or source-specific populations. One supporting-citation gap remains for exact CMHC methodology metadata. These findings establish reported consumer experiences and a dated household count; they do not establish broker shortages or guaranteed recruiting/business outcomes.

Resolution: original CMHC 2026 PDF linked in report and notes. Its p.3 methodology states 4,112 respondents, January 7–February 1, 2026, primary decision-makers18+, mortgage transaction in past18months.
