Mortgage
Conversation Guide.

The goal is not to pitch a mortgage. It is to find out whether a review would be useful.

Start with one question

“When is your mortgage coming up for renewal?”Useful for homeowners who may not have reviewed alternatives.
“Are you comfortable with your current monthly payments?”Opens a conversation about cash flow without promising savings.
“Any plans to move, renovate or make a large purchase?”Helps clarify upcoming financing needs.
“Have you looked at the full cost of refinancing?”Creates room to discuss penalties, fees and repayment length.

Listen for the real goal

“My renewal is coming.”

“When is it due, and have you had a chance to compare your options?”

“I'm paying too much.”

“Is the main concern the monthly payment or the overall cost?”

“I'm just looking.”

“Of course. Would a no-pressure review help you understand what's available?”

“I already have a broker.”

“That's good. Have they walked you through the total costs and alternatives?”

Close with a clear handoff

“If you'd like, I can introduce you to a mortgage specialist. They can review the numbers with you, and you can decide what makes sense.”

Follow-up checklist

Ask permission → Make the introduction → Confirm contact → Check back. If someone asks about joining the business, offer a separate conversation with a team leader.
