More Ways to Help.
A Simple Introduction.

You do not need to solve a mortgage question yourself. Start the conversation, connect the client with a qualified specialist and stay involved.

Three steps to a useful introduction

01 · AskUnderstand the client's goal and timing.
02 · IntroduceWith permission, connect them to the mortgage specialist.
03 · Follow throughConfirm the introduction and check back.
Keep the handoff simple.
“Would it be helpful to speak with someone who can review the mortgage options and explain the numbers?”

When to start the conversation

Buying or moving

Ask about timing, down payment and whether financing has been reviewed.

Renewal coming up

Ask when the term ends and whether the client has compared options.

Monthly payments feel tight

Ask what payment pressure they want to reduce and what trade-offs matter.

Thinking about refinancing

Ask about the goal, then refer for a review of costs, penalties and total borrowing.

Professional boundaries

Do not promise approval, rates, savings or eligibility. Do not collect documents or share personal details without permission. The licensed mortgage professional provides product-specific advice and disclosure.

Better Questions.
Better Mortgage Conversations.

The goal is not to pitch a mortgage. It is to find out whether a review would be useful.

Start with one question

“When is your mortgage coming up for renewal?”Useful for homeowners who may not have reviewed alternatives.
“Are you comfortable with your current monthly payments?”Opens a conversation about cash flow without promising savings.
“Any plans to move, renovate or make a large purchase?”Helps clarify upcoming financing needs.
“Have you looked at the full cost of refinancing?”Creates room to discuss penalties, fees and repayment length.

Listen for the real goal

“My renewal is coming.”

“When is it due, and have you had a chance to compare your options?”

“I'm paying too much.”

“Is the main concern the monthly payment or the overall cost?”

“I'm just looking.”

“Of course. Would a no-pressure review help you understand what's available?”

“I already have a broker.”

“That's good. Have they walked you through the total costs and alternatives?”

Close with a clear handoff

“If you'd like, I can introduce you to a mortgage specialist. They can review the numbers with you, and you can decide what makes sense.”

Follow-up checklist

Confirm consent to introduce → Send the introduction → Confirm contact was made → Follow up on the client's experience, not their private application details.
