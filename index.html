A FEW BETTER QUESTIONS

Mortgage
Conversation Guide.

You don't need a perfect script. Start with what matters to the person. Listen first. Then decide whether an introduction would help.

Where to start

HOMEOWNER / RENEWAL“When does your mortgage come up for renewal?”

If it's coming soon: “Have you compared your options yet?”

PAYMENTS / CASH FLOW“Are you comfortable with what you're paying each month?”

Ask whether their concern is the monthly payment or total cost.

BUYING / MOVING“Any plans to buy, move or renovate?”

Ask about their timing. Let a mortgage specialist discuss financing.

REFINANCING / DEBT“What's the main thing you're hoping to change?”

Listen for the goal. Don't suggest a loan before reviewing the trade-offs.

When they respond

“I'M JUST LOOKING.”That's okay.

“What are you trying to figure out? Maybe I can connect you with someone who can answer that.”

“I ALREADY HAVE A BROKER.”Respect the relationship.

“That's good. Have they had a chance to walk you through your options and the total costs?”

IF SOMEONE ASKS ABOUT JOININGMake it a separate conversation.

“There's a business side to this too. If you're curious, I can introduce you to a team leader who can walk you through the work and the numbers.”

THE PERSONAL INTRODUCTION

“I've got a mortgage specialist I work with. Would you be open to a conversation so you can see what options you have?”

Then follow through.

Ask permission → Make the introduction → Confirm they connected → Check back. You own the relationship. The licensed mortgage specialist handles advice, rates, applications and eligibility.

Do not promise approval, quote mortgage rates, recommend products or request confidential underwriting details. A clear “no” is an answer to respect.
