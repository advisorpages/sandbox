AGENT TOOL / 01

Use during a real conversation

Mortgage
Conversation Guide.

Don't memorize a pitch. Find out what the person is trying to do, then decide whether a personal introduction would help.

Where to start

Renewal“When does your mortgage come up for renewal?”

If soon: “Have you compared your options yet?”

Monthly payments“Are you comfortable with what you're paying each month?”

Ask if their concern is the payment or the full cost.

Buying or moving“Any plans to buy or move in the next year?”

Ask about timing, not a mortgage approval.

Debt / refinancing“What's the main thing you're hoping to change?”

Listen for the goal. Let the specialist weigh the trade-offs.

When they respond

“I'm just looking.”“That's fine. What are you trying to figure out?”

Offer an introduction only if they want help.

“I already have a broker.”“That's good. Is there anything you're still trying to work out?”

Respect their relationship and their answer.

“Who is 8Twelve?”“It's a licensed mortgage brokerage with access to different lenders.”

Explain that your specialist is someone you know. Don't promise the lowest rate.

Asks about joining the teamKeep this separate.

Offer a discussion with a business leader about work, training and qualifications. Don't pivot a client's mortgage need into a pitch.

Personal introduction

“I know a licensed mortgage specialist at 8Twelve. Would you be open to me introducing you so they can review your options?”

Follow through: Ask permission to share a name and chosen contact detail, make the introduction, confirm the connection and agree on a date: “Would Thursday work for me to check back?”

Do not quote rates, promise approval, recommend loans or request confidential underwriting records. 8Twelve is a separate licensed mortgage brokerage. Respect a clear “no.”
